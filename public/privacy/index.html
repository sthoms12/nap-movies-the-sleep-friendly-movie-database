Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

NapMovies is a free, independent, non-commercial catalog of sleep-friendly movie recommendations. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no advertising, and no cookies.

What stays on your device

NapMovies has no accounts and does not store anything you type.

Analytics (Dataflare)

This site uses Dataflare, an analytics tool we self-host in our own Cloudflare account. It is cookieless — it sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. It collects pageviews, the referring site, basic device and browser information, and anonymous interaction events, so we can understand which content is useful. Analytics data is kept for 90 days and is never sold or shared.

Hosting

The site is delivered through Cloudflare, whose infrastructure processes standard request information such as IP address, requested URL, and browser details to deliver and protect the site.

External links

Links to other websites are governed by those websites' own privacy policies.

Children

The site is a general-audience resource and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
